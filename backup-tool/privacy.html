Backup tool: privacy policy

This page covers the backup tool only. It is not the privacy policy or the terms for the Kobci product or for kobci.so visitors.

Who uses the tool

Only its operator, Kobci, signed in with the operator's own Google account. No other person can use it.

What it accesses

The tool asks Google for one permission: to see, create, change and delete only the files that the tool itself creates in the operator's Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in that Drive and it accesses no other Google service.

What it stores

Backup files that are encrypted on the operator's computer before upload. Google stores them in the operator's own Drive. The sign-in token is kept on the operator's computer.

What it collects from other people

Nothing. The tool has no users other than its operator, so it collects, uses and shares no personal data of visitors, customers or anyone else. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

Data handled by the tool is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting

Backups are kept until the operator deletes them. The operator can remove the tool's access at any time in the Google Account security settings, and can delete the backup files in Google Drive.

Contact

Use the contact option on the kobci.so home page.

Effective 11 November 2026.
